Three problems visible from above:

1. The wing openings are 1150 units down the corridor, but the wing rooms centre level with the atrium — so walking in always doglegs.

2. Two large walls sit between the corridor and each wing doing nothing but carrying the brass signs — they are what reads as floating slabs.

3. The details hall has its own side walls that overlap the corridor’s, so two sets of geometry occupy the same space.
