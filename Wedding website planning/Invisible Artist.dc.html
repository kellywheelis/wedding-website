Eight years of collecting, hung for the first time at Villa Cetinale in Sovicille, Italy. Two wings, five days of open hours. Every object in it is something one of us loves. The only element still missing is you.

Wing I · the ceremony

after Botticelli, The Birth of Venus, c. 1485

She arrives on the water, and everyone is already waiting.

Wind on the left, land on the right, and the whole composition holding its breath for an arrival. The painting hangs an hour up the road in Florence.

Wing II · the reception

after Botticelli, Primavera, c. 1480

Then the garden comes indoors and nobody goes home.

Tables dressed as banquet still life: figs, pomegranates, spilled candle wax, far too many flowers. Botticelli put a hundred and ninety species of plant in one painting. We are attempting fewer.
