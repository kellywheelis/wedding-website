Plate II

The Vows

Written by both of us, read once, never rehearsed. Anthony maintains he will not cry.

Ink on paper · 2027

The reference isn’t the sea. It’s her.

Botticelli gave a woman the entire centre of the canvas, in gold light, with flowers in the air and nobody hurrying her. That is the tone of the ceremony — femininity taken completely seriously. Held in the Tuscan hills, nowhere near any water.

Between the two wings there is a dark corridor, and it is deliberate.

One collection ends. Your eyes adjust. The next begins in a completely different key.
