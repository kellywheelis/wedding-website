The guests’ photographs

The link on the sign at the villa

Open the upload page

Print the QR code on the sign: it opens the upload page, and nobody without it can add photographs. Until it opens, only you can add, from a device that has opened this page. If the link is ever shared where it should not be, make a new one and reprint the sign: the old one stops working at once.

The guest list

Copy it from your spreadsheet and paste it here: one household to a row, with the columns Names, Phone numbers (several in one cell are fine, separated by commas), Seats, and Events (the names, or words from them, separated by commas; or all). A header row is fine. Each household is given its own code; to keep a code you like, add a Code column. Loading replaces the whole list, but a household keeps its code, QR code and reply as long as it keeps its names, its code, or one of its phone numbers.
