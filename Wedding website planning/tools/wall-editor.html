Selected

Click a photograph (or the plaque) to size and frame it. Drag to move; the arrow keys nudge it 1 cm (with Shift, 5 cm). Pink lines show when it lines up with another.

Your photographs

Click one to put it on the wall or take it off. Outlined: on the wall.

Blank frames

Placeholders for photographs still to come: add as many as you like, then size, frame and arrange them. They hang as empty frames until a photograph takes their place.

When you are happy

Press Save to the gallery, then reload the gallery (open it) and walk to the wall. Nothing is live on the website until you say commit.
